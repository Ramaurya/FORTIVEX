# NovaDesk Features and Integrations

## AI auto-resolution bot
The AI bot answers common customer questions (order status, refund policy, timings, FAQs) automatically 24/7 using your own help articles. It hands over to a human agent when it is not confident or when the customer asks for a human.

## AI reply suggestions
For every ticket, NovaDesk suggests a ready-to-send reply in the customer's language. Agents can edit and send with one click. Supports 30+ languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Arabic and Spanish.

## Sentiment detection and priority
NovaDesk detects frustrated or angry customers in real time and moves their tickets to the top of the queue.

## Omnichannel inbox
Email, WhatsApp Business, website live chat widget, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger and (Enterprise) phone calls all in one inbox.

## Integrations
Native integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Slack, Microsoft Teams and Razorpay. Open REST API and webhooks for custom integrations. Setup for most integrations takes under 10 minutes.

## Reports and analytics
Dashboards for first response time, resolution time, CSAT, agent performance, ticket volume by channel, and AI resolution rate. Reports can be exported to Excel or scheduled by email.

## Onboarding
Starter and Growth customers get free guided onboarding over video call and can go live in 1 to 3 days. Enterprise customers get a dedicated onboarding manager and data migration from Zendesk, Freshdesk or Zoho Desk.
