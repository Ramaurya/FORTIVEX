# NovaDesk Pricing and Plans

## Starter plan
Price: 999 INR per agent per month (billed annually) or 1,199 INR per agent per month (billed monthly). Includes shared inbox for email and website chat, 1,000 AI-assisted replies per month, basic reports, and email support. Minimum 2 agents.

## Growth plan
Price: 2,499 INR per agent per month (billed annually) or 2,999 INR monthly. Includes everything in Starter plus WhatsApp and Instagram channels, unlimited AI-suggested replies, AI auto-resolution bot, ticket routing rules, CRM integrations (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce), and priority chat support. Most popular plan.

## Enterprise plan
Custom pricing, usually starting around 4,500 INR per agent per month for 50+ agents. Includes everything in Growth plus voice/phone channel, custom AI model trained on your data, single sign-on (SSO), dedicated account manager, 99.9 percent uptime SLA, and on-premise or private cloud deployment option.

## Discounts
Annual billing saves about 17 percent. Startups under 2 years old and registered NGOs get an extra 20 percent discount. Educational institutions get 25 percent off the Growth plan.

## Free trial
Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Trial includes all Growth plan features.

## Payment and refunds
We accept credit cards, UPI, net banking and bank transfer for annual plans. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans get a pro-rated refund if cancelled within the first 30 days.
