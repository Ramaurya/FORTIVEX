# NovaDesk FAQ - Security, Support and Common Questions

## Data security
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). NovaDesk is ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type II compliant. We are compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR. Customer data is hosted in AWS Mumbai region by default; EU hosting is available for Enterprise.

## Does the AI use our data to train public models
No. Your data is never used to train public AI models. Enterprise customers can get a private AI model trained only on their own data.

## Uptime and reliability
Growth and Starter plans have 99.5 percent uptime. Enterprise has a 99.9 percent uptime SLA with service credits.

## Customer support for NovaDesk users
Starter: email support within 24 hours. Growth: priority chat support within 2 hours. Enterprise: 24/7 phone support plus dedicated account manager.

## Can we migrate from another helpdesk
Yes. We provide free migration of tickets, contacts and help articles from Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk and Intercom.

## Mobile app
Yes, NovaDesk has Android and iOS apps for agents.

## Number of users
There is no limit on number of customers you support. Pricing is per agent (team member who replies to tickets).

## Demo
A free 30-minute live demo with a product specialist can be booked Monday to Friday between 10 AM and 6 PM IST. The demo is personalised to the customer's business and use-case.
