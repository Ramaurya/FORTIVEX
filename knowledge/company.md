# NovaTech Solutions - Company Overview

## About NovaTech
NovaTech Solutions is a software company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with offices in Pune and Dubai. We build NovaDesk, a cloud-based customer support helpdesk powered by AI. More than 1,200 businesses use NovaDesk, mainly in e-commerce, education, healthcare clinics, real estate and SaaS.

## What NovaDesk does
NovaDesk brings all customer conversations (email, WhatsApp, website live chat, Instagram and phone) into one shared inbox. An AI assistant suggests replies, auto-answers common questions, tags and routes tickets to the right team, and detects angry customers so they are handled first. Managers get reports on response time, resolution time and customer satisfaction (CSAT).

## Ideal customers
NovaDesk is best for businesses with 3 to 500 support agents who receive at least 500 customer queries per month and struggle with slow replies, missed messages across channels, or high support staff costs.

## Results customers see
On average customers report 40 percent faster first response time, 30 percent of tickets fully resolved by AI without a human, and a 15 point increase in CSAT within three months.
