# Sales Playbook - Handling Common Objections

## It is too expensive
Explain the return on investment: the AI bot resolves around 30 percent of tickets automatically, which typically saves the cost of one support agent for every 4 to 5 agents. Mention annual billing saves 17 percent, startup and NGO discounts, and that the Starter plan begins at 999 INR per agent per month. Offer the free 14-day trial.

## We already use another tool like Zendesk or Freshdesk
NovaDesk offers stronger support for Indian languages and WhatsApp, pricing in INR that is usually 30 to 40 percent lower than global tools, data hosted in India, and free migration. Suggest a side-by-side demo.

## We need to think about it or I need approval
Respect it. Offer to book a short demo where the decision maker can join, or send information by email. Ask when would be a good time to follow up.

## Worried about AI giving wrong answers
The AI only answers from the customer's own approved help articles, shows confidence scores, and hands over to a human when unsure. Admins control which topics the bot can answer.

## Worried about data security
NovaDesk is ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified, DPDP Act and GDPR compliant, and data is stored in India (AWS Mumbai) by default.

## Implementation will take too long
Most customers go live in 1 to 3 days with free guided onboarding.
